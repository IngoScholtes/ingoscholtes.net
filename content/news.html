  1. New preprint on node features in graph pooling

    We have posted a new preprint titled The Role of Node Features in Graph Pooling on arXiv, co-authored with Jan von Pichowski, Alžbeta Hrabošová, and Christopher Blöcker. In this work, we systematically investigate how node features interact with graph pooling mechanisms in graph neural networks. May 11, 2026

  2. New preprint on neural operator surrogates for black hole simulations

    We have posted a new preprint titled Learning Neural Operator Surrogates for the Black Hole Accretion Code on arXiv, co-authored with Matthias Naegele, Cedric Boes, Chester Tan, Christian M. Fromm, and Karl Mannheim. In this interdisciplinary work, we apply machine learning to build fast surrogate models for numerical simulations of black hole accretion. April 30, 2026

  4. Paper published in TMLR

    We are happy to announce that our work From Link Prediction to Forecasting: Addressing Challenges in Batch-based Temporal Graph Learning has now been published in the journal Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR). In our work, we highlight fallacies in the common batch-based evaluation of link prediction methods for temporal graphs. February 20, 2026
